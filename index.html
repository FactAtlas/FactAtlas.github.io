About

Fact Atlas is a YouTube channel that publishes short videos about surprising but true facts — from the deep ocean and outer space to ancient history and the human body.

Videos are produced with the help of automated tools: scripts are drafted with AI assistance, narrated with synthetic voices and illustrated with licensed stock footage from Pixabay.

Fact Atlas Uploader

Fact Atlas Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the Fact Atlas channel, set the channel banner and read view statistics of the channel's own videos. It does not access data of any other YouTube user.
